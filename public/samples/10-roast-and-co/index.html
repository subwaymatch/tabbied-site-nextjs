The roast

What is on the bag

If a roaster will not print this, there is usually a reason for it.

Roast date

Printed, not a best before. Everything ships within four days of roasting.

Price paid

The farmgate price per kilo, on every bag. It averages 2.7 times the fair trade floor.

Producer

Named farm or co-op, altitude, and varietal. No blends sold as single origins.

Roast level

A measured number rather than the word medium. Most of ours sit between 58 and 72.

Process

Washed, natural, or honey, stated plainly, with fermentation time where we know it.

Packaging

Home compostable, with a valve that composts too, which was the hard part.
