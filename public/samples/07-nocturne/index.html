Composition

What is in the bottle

The full pyramid and the concentration, printed on every carton.

Concentration

Extrait at 24 percent. It lasts because of the oil load, not because of a fixative.

Top

Bergamot, pink pepper, and a green note lifted from crushed fig leaf.

Heart

Orris butter, jasmine absolute, and a small amount of leather underneath.

Base

Sandalwood, ambrette seed, and vetiver from a single farm in Haiti.

Naturals

Eighty-two percent by weight. The rest is there because nature cannot do it safely.

Maceration

Six weeks in steel before bottling. Nothing ships the week it was mixed.

The composition

Built around one accord

Each scent begins with a single idea and a few materials, layered so it opens, turns, and settles like a piece of music.

The maturation

Rested before it is bottled

Every batch sits for weeks so the materials marry, then it is decanted into refillable glass by hand.

Composed for night

Deeper materials that speak after dark.

Extrait strength

High concentration, long on the skin.
