In the studio

How a pot happens

Five weeks from wet clay to your table, and most of that is spent waiting.

01

Throw

Stoneware from a quarry in Devon, thrown wet, one at a time, on a kick wheel.

02

Dry slow

Two weeks under plastic. Rush this and it cracks, so we do not rush it.

03

Bisque

A first firing to 1,000 degrees, which is where about a tenth of them fail.

04

Glaze and fire

Dipped by hand, then a long firing to 1,280 with a slow cool for the colour.
