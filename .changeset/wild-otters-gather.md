Add 200 new generative designs (gallery orders 700+), bringing the preset
catalog to 314.

Fifty-six of them are written directly against the vocabulary of the eleven
artworks Syung Hong drew by hand — Radius, Mixtape, Odessa, Symmetry, Veil,
Blossom, Disque, Bloks, Terrain, Trigram and Ring: one shape per cell, its
outline rolled out of a short hand-picked library rather than a continuum,
a single ink sampled per cell, and shapes allowed to break out of their cell
the way Odessa's pills run three rows tall and Ring's circles slide out from
under each other. Each of those entries names the original it descends from.
The rest take the same machinery to heraldry, mouldings and openings, cloth,
type and print, notation, the sky, survey drawings, stone, botany, machined
parts, folded paper, and the girih / zellige / komon tiling traditions.

Every design in the batch is background-independent: no rule paints
`var(--color0)`, so every gap is real geometry (a clip-path hole, a mask, a
border, or a gap between two elements) and the design renders identically over
any background, including a transparent one.

Ten of them also ship the ToggleSwitch options the originals use — a Shadow
switch (seven designs) and a Rounded Corners switch (three) — which the batch
tooling now supports.
